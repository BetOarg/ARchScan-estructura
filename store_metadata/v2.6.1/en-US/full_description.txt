ARchScan helps capture indoor and outdoor spaces from a mobile device. Depending on device capabilities, it uses augmented reality, a conventional camera, or a safe manual mode.

Measure walls, doors, and windows; connect rooms; organize the complete floor plan; and save projects to continue later. Edit measurements, use metric or imperial units, and identify shared walls.

Import and export projects as JSON and create technical PDF documents containing plans, dimensions, areas, and perimeters.

Main features:
• Automatic selection between AR, conventional camera, and manual scanning.
• Measurement and editing of rooms, walls, doors, and windows.
• Dimensioned 2D plans and room organization.
• Local projects and optional account synchronization.
• Recovery and compatibility with historical projects.
• JSON import/export and technical PDF documents.
• Spanish and English interface.
• Metric and imperial units.

ARchScan is a surveying aid. Verify measurements before using them for construction documents, estimates, or professional decisions.
